# The 2026-11-14 verdict: what changed and why

Three independent optimization studies were run on this bot on 14 November 2026 and then audited against each other
(project doc `claude/apex-chronos-final-verdict-three-studies-2026-11-14.md`):

| Study | Search | Best candidate | Why it was rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, 43 SIP sessions | 3,984 strategy rows + 51,840 account overlays | joint-0596 + VIX 15-25 gate | Under stressed costs all of its profit came from trades priced on same-day expiries that are not listed (COIN/MSTR are Friday-only, the other single names Mon/Wed/Fri); 97% of its last-window gain was one COIN trade |
| B, 351 IEX sessions, pre-registered | about 90 variables, 3 stages, 7 gates, walk-forward | midday P2 carry | Model IV is about 2.2-2.4x realised rest-of-day vol; under fair IV the walk-forward lost 0.95% of the account per session, and random entry minutes did as well as the P2 signal |
| C, 222 configs, 6 gates | exits, entries, portfolio, friction | P2 max hold 80 -> 112 min | With only listed same-day contracts and IV x 0.65 the gain is +0.34 (p 0.17) and negative on the core sample |

No signal, exit, threshold, ticker or slot rule is changed. Every module still runs, and every model trade is still
written to `ledger/trades.csv` exactly as before (the verification harness still matches the original backtest 110/110).
The Phase 2 keep-or-retire rule (`docs/P2_DECISION.md`, `phase_review.RULE`) is untouched and starts on 2026-11-16 as frozen.

## What the bot now does (apex_chronos/verdict.py, on in production via `Settings.from_env`)

1. **P1 and P3 are retired from alerting in code.** Setting `MUTED_PHASES=none` no longer brings them back; changing
   `verdict.RETIRED_PHASES` (a reviewed code change) would. They are still evaluated and logged every evening.
   They also still occupy paper slots in the engine exactly as before, so the P2 trades the rule judges are the same
   trades the rule was designed on. Freeing those slots for P2 is a candidate change for after the P2 verdict.
2. **A P2 entry is alerted only if a same-day expiry is listed** for that ticker, checked against the morning Nasdaq
   options directory snapshot (`state/cash_options/`, written by the `cash-option-catalog` job). With no snapshot it
   falls back to the observed schedule: SPY/QQQ/IWM daily; NVDA/TSLA/META/AMD/AAPL/MSFT/AMZN Mon/Wed/Fri;
   COIN/MSTR Friday. A withheld entry gets a one-line ⚪ notice and stays in the paper ledger.
3. **P2 is re-graded at a fair implied vol every evening** into `ledger/p2_grading.csv`: same entry, strikes rule and
   exit ladder, with the IV path scaled so that IV at entry = 1.2 x a causal forecast of rest-of-day realised vol
   (mean same-window realised variance over the last 14 archived sessions; needs at least 5). Each row also records
   whether a same-day expiry was listed. The evening post shows model, listed-only, fair-vol and listed + fair-vol means.
   **The P2 rule still judges the model column**; these columns are the evidence for what happens after February.
4. **Kill switch.** Each evening, model equity of the alerted phases (from 2026-11-16, at the slot fraction) is
   checked. If it is more than 15% below its high-water mark, or the latest session lost 3% or more, every alert is
   muted the next session (`state/kill-switch.json`). It clears by itself at the first evening review where neither holds.
5. **Default sizing is 10% of the account per slot** (was 25%; 3 slots = at most 30% in same-day options). The repo
   variable `SLOT_FRACTION` still overrides it. At a $1,500 account no P2 spread fits (median collateral is about $560
   a contract), so alerts will say "0 contracts"; that is intended: P2 is a paper alert until real quotes prove it.
6. **Raw bar archive.** The evening job saves the session's final one-minute bars and VIX to `state/bars/` (about
   60 KB a day). Yahoo keeps only about 30 days; this is what makes the studies repeatable. Seeded with
   16 Oct - 13 Nov 2026 from the Yahoo data used in the studies.
7. **Quote trial identity refreshed** (`trial/cash_quote_trial.json` -> `cash_quotes_p1a_v2_20261114`, observation-only,
   starting 2026-11-16) because the Tradier-feed merge changed `data.py` and the frozen source hash no longer matched.
   Nothing old is relabelled as qualified evidence; risk limits, size-unit confirmation and promotion stay unapproved.

## Backfill: the existing P2 record at fair vol

`ledger/p2_grading_backfill_2026-10-16_2026-11-13.csv` re-grades the 39 P2 ledger trades that the archived bars cover
(35 reproduce the ledger exactly at scale 1; the other 4 differ because the final-bar data differs slightly).

| Subset | Model mean | Fair-vol mean |
|---|---|---|
| 30 trades with at least 5 archived prior sessions | +4.49% | **+0.82%** |
| ...of which a same-day expiry was listed (21) | +3.27% | **+0.22%** |
| All 39: listed (30) vs not listed (9) | +3.35% vs +7.32% | |

At fair vol, P2's own forward record sits below the rule's +1.0% KEEP floor, and the model's best P2 trades were on
contracts that did not exist. That is a warning, not a verdict: the binding decision is still the frozen rule on
the first 100 trades from 16 November.
